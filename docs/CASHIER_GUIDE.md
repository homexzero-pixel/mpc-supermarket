# Scan & Shop — cashier guide

**Sign in:** `/staff` on the counter computer. The **Orders** screen updates by itself and beeps for new baskets and printer problems (click once on the page to allow sound).

## A customer arrives with a slip or a code on their phone

1. **Scan the slip barcode** into the box at the top (or type the code, e.g. `M042`) and press **Open**.
2. Press **Open at my counter**. Now only you can change or bill this basket.
3. **Check the items** in the customer's basket against the list:
   - Customer put something back → change the quantity or press **Remove**.
   - Customer picked up something extra → scan its barcode under **Add missed item** (or search by name).
   - **Loose produce** (yellow rows) → weigh it and enter the grams, then **Set weight**. Payment buttons stay locked until every loose item is weighed.
4. Press **Cash**, **UPI**, **Card** or **Other**. The POS works out the final amount and prints the bill. The order turns **Paid** only when the POS confirms.

## If something goes wrong

| You see | Do this |
|---|---|
| "The POS did not confirm. Do NOT charge again" | Press **Check payment**. If the POS has the payment, the order becomes Paid. If not, it tells you it is safe to try again. |
| "Not in POS yet" | The basket is still being sent to the billing system. Wait a few seconds; a manager can use **Reconcile**. |
| **PRINT FAILED** | Fix the printer (power, paper, cable), then **Retry print**. |
| **Print unconfirmed** | Check the printer. If there's no slip, **Reprint COPY** and give a reason. |
| Another cashier has the order | Ask them, or a manager can **Take over**. |
| Customer wants to pay at another counter | **Move to counter** — a slip prints there and the customer's phone updates. |
| Customer left without paying | **Cancel order** with a reason. Unpaid baskets also expire automatically. |

Never ask a customer to submit their basket again because of a printer problem — the order is already here.
